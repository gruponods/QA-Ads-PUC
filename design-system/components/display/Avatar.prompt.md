Person marker for students, teachers and the signed-in user. Initials in blue on the `#EEF2FE` tint when there is no photo.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Laura Méndez" />
<Avatar name="Tomás Ibarra" size={48} src="/fotos/tomas.jpg" />
```

Common sizes: 28 (dense lists) · 36 (tables, sidebar) · 64 (profile).
