The eyebrow above a title. 13px Medium, uppercase, wide tracking, with a short blue accent tick. Use on covers, section dividers, and slide headers to categorize the slide.

```jsx
<Kicker>Plataforma NODS</Kicker>
<Kicker color="#fff" accent={false}>Capítulo 02</Kicker>
```

Notes:
- Keep kickers short (1–3 words). They set context; the title carries the message.
- On dark dividers pass `color="#fff"`. The accent tick stays brand blue.
